4 · CORRECT

Fix the layer by hand. Corrections are saved separately from detections.geojson, which the pipeline overwrites, so they survive re-running detection and can be folded into the training labels.
Pen — hold the mouse down and trace round an array, freehand. The map stops panning while the pen is active.
Eraser — drag across a blue shape to mark it wrong and remove it, or across something you drew to rub it out. To bring a removed shape back, click its red outline.
Edit shape — click any outline, then drag its points to move them, drag a midpoint to add one, or right-click a point to delete it. Click the map when done.
Click points — vertex-by-vertex polygon, for straight edges.
Undo last — takes back the most recent correction.
Esc cancels and returns to panning.
Save stores your edits. Apply folds them into the labels and rebuilds the layer, so drawn arrays become measured shapes like the rest. Takes a few minutes — it re-traces on CPU.
